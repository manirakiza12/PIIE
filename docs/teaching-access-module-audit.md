## Lifecycle and governance

The existing service remains the authority for activation, effective dates, eligibility, institutional boundaries, conflicts and audit records. Planned appointments activate only when valid. Cancelled/ended appointments are never resurrected. Closed offerings stop operational teaching access. No permanent per-user capability rows, schema migrations, meetings, marks, attendance or messages are generated by allocation.

Examination approval and publication, result release, fee checks and registration confirmation remain governed operations. A teaching allocation does not approve an examination, publish results, waive fees or confirm student enrolment. Extending teaching rights to assistants/guests, group-history visibility, a general announcement editor and broader progress analytics need explicit institutional decisions; they are not automatically enabled for those roles.

## Verification

Regression coverage includes automatic activation and reconciliation, Primary/Co-Lecturer workspace access, Meet Now 403 and selector behaviour, content/assignments/attendance/assessments, messaging in both directions, unconfirmed students, unrelated/foreign lecturers, future/expired/cancelled appointments, retained message history and queued portal notifications. Exam regression verifies an author loses management/attempt access after cancellation, end or expiry while the exam record remains.

Private conversations are pair-scoped, not separate conversations for each course. This deliberately reuses existing infrastructure. History stays with the original participants under the existing retention model. No deployment or production mutation is part of this implementation.
